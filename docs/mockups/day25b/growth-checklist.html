How the Growth checklist works (design notes, not on the page)
  • A checklist, not a second plan. 4–6 habits a week (the seasonal idea only when a holiday is within ~6 weeks). No replies, listing details or hours here: those are Fix plan's.
  • Checking off plays the old celebration: a confetti burst at the box, and when the week first counts, the trophy toast. Reduce-motion skips the confetti.
  • Auto-checks what PostScore can see (connected): the post went out, a new photo, an offer started in PostScore. The owner can still tick anything by hand.
  • The tier reads these check-offs (an active week). "Also helps your score" marks habits that feed a Fix plan outcome (reviews, photos), without repeating the fix.
  • Resets Monday in the business's own time zone. Click any box here to try the animation.
